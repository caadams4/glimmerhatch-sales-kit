# Harbourline Distribution: billing exceptions cut by more than half

**Sector:** Wholesale distribution
**Size:** About 650 staff, two warehouses
**Sprint length:** 3 weeks
**Pod / team size:** 4 consultants (Pod Hobby)

## Where they were

Around one invoice in nine bounced back from customers: wrong delivery
reference, wrong rate card, missing proof of delivery. Finance spent most of
every month chasing them.

## What we did

We sampled six months of exceptions and sorted them by cause. Three causes
accounted for most of the volume. We fixed the data at source (the delivery
reference was being retyped by hand at the warehouse) and wrote a weekly
exception report that went to the warehouse leads, not to finance.

## What changed

Exceptions fell from roughly 11% of invoices to roughly 5% within two months
of the sprint ending.

## In their words

"Finance stopped dreading month end." (approved)
