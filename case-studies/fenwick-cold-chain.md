# Fenwick Cold Chain: seeing temperature breaches before the customer does

**Sector:** Chilled and frozen food logistics
**Size:** About 220 staff, one hub, 60 vehicles
**Sprint length:** 6 weeks
**Pod / team size:** 6 consultants

## Where they were

Temperature loggers on the vehicles produced a file at the end of each run.
Breaches were found the next morning, usually because a customer rang.

## What we did

We wired the logger exports into one place, agreed with the transport
manager what counted as a breach, and built an alert view that updated
through the day. The last two weeks were spent on the awkward part: getting
drivers and planners to actually use it.

## What changed

Time from breach to first call-out dropped from "next morning" to under 40
minutes on most runs.
