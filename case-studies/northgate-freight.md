# Northgate Freight: dock turnaround down by a fifth in four weeks

**Sector:** Regional road freight
**Size:** About 400 staff, four depots in the Midlands
**Sprint length:** 4 weeks
**Pod / team size:** 5 consultants

## Where they were

Northgate's depots booked dock slots on a shared calendar and a lot of
phone calls. Lorries waited, drivers ran out of hours, and nobody could say
which depot was actually the slow one.

## What we did

Week one was mapping how a slot was really booked, as opposed to how the
process document said it was. Weeks two and three built a simple scheduling
model on their own booking data and a dashboard the depot managers could
read on a phone. Week four was handover and training.

## What changed

Average dock turnaround fell from roughly 62 minutes to roughly 49.
