# Calder Wharf Hauliers: one planner, one screen, fewer missed slots

**Sector:** Small-fleet haulage
**Size:** About 70 staff, 25 vehicles
**Sprint length:** 2 weeks
**Pod / team size:** 3 consultants

## Where they were

Planning lived in one person's head and one very large spreadsheet. When
that person was off, loads were missed.

## What we did

Two weeks. Week one: sit with the planner and write down what she actually
decides each morning. Week two: put those decisions into a shared planning
sheet with checks, and train a second person to run it.

## What changed

Missed delivery slots fell from about eight a month to about two. More
importantly, the planner took a holiday.

## In their words

"I took a week off and nothing broke." (approved)
