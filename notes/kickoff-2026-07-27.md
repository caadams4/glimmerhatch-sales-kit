# Kickoff call, Mon 27 July

On the call: me, two people from StrikeOne Commercial, someone from Delivery
who dropped off at the half hour.

What they said they want:

- One pitch they can reuse for mid-market logistics bids (road freight,
  3PLs, distribution). Not a brochure.
- A deck of about 12 slides, plus a leave-behind they can email.
- Three or four case studies. They are fine with clients being anonymised
  but want the numbers real-ish. (We agreed: names changed, figures rounded.)
- Sprints are the hook. Two to six weeks, fixed scope, day rate. Lead with
  that, because logistics buyers hate open-ended consulting.

My worries:
- Their existing logistics material is thin. We may be inventing structure
  rather than polishing it.
- Timeline is "before the end of October". Fine, but the case studies need
  client sign-off and that always takes longest.
