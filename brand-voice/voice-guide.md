# Voice guide (first pass)

StrikeOne's sales material, historically, has read like a procurement
spreadsheet that learned to talk. Our job is to keep the rigour and lose
the stiffness.

## The short version

Write the way a sensible partner would explain things over coffee to a
logistics director who has eleven minutes and a delayed pallet to deal with.

## What we want

- Plain words. "Use" rather than "utilise", "start" rather than "commence".
- Numbers early. If a result had a number, the number goes in the first
  sentence, not the fourth paragraph.
- The client is the subject of the sentence. "Northgate cut dock turnaround
  by a fifth", not "A reduction in dock turnaround was achieved".
- Short paragraphs. Three sentences is plenty.

## What we do not want

- Anything that begins "In today's fast-paced supply chain environment".
- Claims we cannot back with an engagement. If we cannot name the sprint, we
  cannot say it.
- Exclamation marks (I know, I know. I use them in emails. Not in the kit.)
