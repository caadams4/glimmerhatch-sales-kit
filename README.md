# Glimmerhatch x StrikeOne: logistics sales kit

Hello, and welcome (whoever you are)!

This is where Glimmerhatch Advisory and the Commercial team at StrikeOne
Consulting built the logistics-sector sales kit between late July and the
end of October 2026. Version 1 was presented to Commercial on 26 October and
went down well. A few small changes are still open (see `notes/v1.1-changes.md`).

I am Lucinda, I look after the bids side at Glimmerhatch, and I would like to
say a proper thank you to the people who read drafts at short notice,
particularly Kofi in the Rotterdam delivery team, who told me, quite rightly,
that logistics people skim.

## What is in here

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `pitch/` | The pitch outline and three drafts. Draft 3 is the latest. |
| `slides/` | Slide outlines plus the working deck export (`sales_deck.pptx`, v1.1 draft with the 3PL slide). `outline-v2.md` is current, and there is a frozen copy of what we presented. |
| `case-studies/` | Four write-ups with invented-for-publication names: Northgate, Harbourline, Fenwick and Calder Wharf. Real clients, changed names, rounded numbers. |
| `brand-voice/` | The voice guide and a word list. Please read these before editing anything. |
| `templates/` | The case-study template. |
| `notes/` | Call notes, rehearsal notes and the change list. Untidy but honest. |

## How to work in here

The designed deck lives in the shared document space (folder "Logistics 2026");
the `slides/sales_deck.pptx` export here is the working copy, so you do not have
to dig through the shared space for it. If
you do change the deck, please change the matching outline too, or ask me
and I will do it.

I do not use git much, I am afraid. If a commit message looks odd, it came
from the desktop client.
